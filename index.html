Research questions & project notes +
01 / LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY

Can people tell where their data goes?

How do interface explanations affect understanding of on-device processing, encrypted storage, and recovery? PhotoMind and ARKIVE offer concrete contexts for exploring this question.

02 / ACCESSIBLE MAP CUES

When colour stops carrying meaning, what works?

Which combinations of colour, shape, line style, and labels help people distinguish routes and hazards under colour-vision differences?

03 / TRUST & RECOVERY

How can irreversible actions become understandable?

How should photo cleanup and encrypted-vault recovery communicate consequences, uncertainty, and the options available before users commit?

SCINTILLA / CUELOCK — PROJECT RESEARCH NOTE

Checking meaning in map cues

Problem: distinct route roles can become ambiguous when colour differences collapse.

Approach: CueLock assigns semantic roles, simulates colour-vision differences, checks encodings, and suggests additional visual cues. Its documented method references Machado et al. (2009), CIEDE2000, and a dual-encoding rule.

Related work: the project documentation places it alongside Color Oracle, Sim Daltonism, TextureMap, and Map Colouriser. Its focus is checking role distinctions and re-verifying changes.

Evaluation and limitations: this is a working research instrument. No completed participant study or peer-reviewed evaluation is claimed here. Simulation and automated checks do not establish real-world accessibility or WCAG certification.

Proposed next step: compare cue encodings in a task-based user study, measuring role recognition, errors, and confidence.
